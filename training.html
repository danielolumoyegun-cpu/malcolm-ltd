Training Introduction

Practical Marketing Knowledge for a
Changing World

Marketing continues to evolve as customer behaviour, digital platforms and artificial intelligence transform how businesses communicate and grow.

Our training programmes are designed to make modern marketing easier to understand and apply. We combine clear instruction, practical exercises and real-world examples to help participants develop skills they can use confidently within businesses, careers and communities.

  • Brand purpose and positioning

Business Development and Lead Generation

Develop practical skills for identifying opportunities, starting conversations and turning marketing interest into potential business.

Topics may include:

  • Understanding ideal customers
  • Prospect research
  • Lead-generation strategies
  • Outreach planning
  • Building professional relationships
  • Partnership development
  • Sales and marketing alignment
  • Managing a lead pipeline

AI for Marketing

Learn how to use artificial intelligence responsibly to make marketing simpler, faster and more effective.

Our AI training remains human-led. Participants learn how to use technology as a supporting tool while maintaining creativity, professional judgement and genuine customer relationships.

Topics may include:

  • Understanding AI in marketing
  • AI-assisted research
  • Content ideation and development
  • Marketing automation
  • Prompt-writing fundamentals
  • Responsible and ethical AI use
  • Customer personalisation
  • Campaign planning and optimisation
  • Protecting accuracy, originality and brand voice

Marketing Analytics and Performance

Learn how to measure marketing activity and use evidence to make better decisions.

Topics may include:

  • Setting marketing objectives
  • Understanding website analytics
  • Selecting relevant performance indicators
  • Tracking campaigns
  • Measuring social-media activity
  • Interpreting customer behaviour
  • Creating clear performance reports
  • Using insights to improve future activity
