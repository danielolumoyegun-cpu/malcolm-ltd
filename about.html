Our Story

Why Malcolm Inc
Exists

Many small and growing businesses have strong ideas, valuable products and ambitious goals but struggle to access the quality of marketing support needed to compete effectively.

Some rely on disconnected freelancers. Others attempt to manage every marketing activity themselves or pay for services that produce activity without clear direction.

Malcolm Inc was created to provide a better way.

We give businesses access to coordinated marketing strategy, creative execution, digital expertise, AI-supported solutions, business-development support and outdoor advertising through one trusted partner.

Our purpose is simple:
To elevate brands and empower businesses to fulfil their potential.

Malcolm Inc is a Middlesbrough-based, digital-first marketing company supporting businesses across Teesside, the North East, the wider United Kingdom and, where appropriate, international markets.

We work as an extension of our clients’ businesses, taking time to understand their vision, customers, challenges and commercial objectives.

Our approach combines personal service with the skills, technology and capabilities businesses would normally expect from a larger marketing department.

We do not believe in marketing simply for the sake of activity. Every strategy, campaign and creative decision should have a clear purpose and contribute to meaningful business progress.

Our mission is to help businesses build, manage and grow their market presence through accessible, creative, technology-enabled and results-focused marketing solutions delivered with a personal touch.

AI and Marketing Innovation

Making Marketing Smarter, Simpler and More Accessible

Malcolm Inc believes artificial intelligence will play an important role in the future of marketing.

We use AI-supported tools to improve research, generate insights, understand customer behaviour, personalise marketing activity and make everyday marketing processes more efficient.

Our approach combines artificial intelligence with human strategy, creativity and commercial judgement. AI supports our work, but the ideas, decisions and relationships behind every project remain human-led.

We aim to help businesses use AI responsibly to:

  • Understand their customers and markets
  • Identify new growth opportunities
  • Improve marketing research and planning
  • Create and organise content more efficiently
  • Personalise customer communications
  • Automate repetitive marketing tasks
  • Analyse campaign performance
  • Improve customer journeys
  • Make faster, evidence-based decisions
  • Reduce the time and cost of managing marketing activity

As Malcolm Inc develops, we plan to create AI-enabled marketing tools and solutions that make professional marketing easier to access, understand and manage, especially for small and growing businesses.

Our ambition is not simply to follow the future of marketing. We want to help shape it.

Meet the Founder

Malik Oluwadamilare
Adebowale-Oke

Founder and Director

Malcolm Inc was founded by Malik Oluwadamilare Adebowale-Oke, a marketing and business-development professional with experience across marketing strategy, digital marketing, customer acquisition, sales, banking, telecommunications and business growth.

Malik holds an MSc in International Business Management and Marketing and has worked with organisations and customers across the United Kingdom and Nigeria.

His experience has shown him that many businesses do not lack ambition or potential. They often lack coordinated marketing support, clear direction and access to the right expertise and technology.

Malcolm Inc was established to help close that gap.
