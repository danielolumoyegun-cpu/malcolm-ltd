Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

This policy explains what personal information Malcolm Inc collects through this website, why we collect it, who it is shared with and what rights you have over it. We are the data controller for the information described here.

Who we are

Malcolm Inc is a marketing agency based in Middlesbrough, Teesside, United Kingdom. If you have any question about this policy or about how your information is handled, contact us at info@malcominc.co.uk.

What we collect

We only collect personal information that you choose to give us. If you complete an enquiry form on this website, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your business or organisation name, if you provide one
  • Your phone number, if you provide one
  • Your business location, if you provide one
  • The service you are interested in, your estimated budget and preferred start date, if you provide them
  • How you heard about us, if you tell us
  • Anything you write in the message field

We do not ask for payment details, identity documents or any special category data through this website. Please do not send such information through our forms.

Why we use it, and our lawful basis

Responding to your enquiry
When you tick the consent box and submit a form, we use your details to reply and to discuss the work you are asking about. Our lawful basis is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.
Managing a working relationship
If you go on to become a client, we use your contact details to deliver and administer the work. Our lawful basis is the performance of our contract with you.
Keeping our own records
We keep a record of enquiries and correspondence. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in running the business and keeping accurate records.

We do not use your information for automated decision-making or profiling, and we do not sell it to anyone.

Marketing

We will not add you to a marketing list simply because you submitted an enquiry. If we ever send marketing emails, we will ask you first and every message will include an unsubscribe link.

Who your information is shared with

We use a small number of service providers to run this website. They process data on our behalf and only for the purposes we set.

Web3Forms
Delivers our website form submissions to our inbox. Everything you type into a form passes through this service before it reaches us.
GitHub Pages
Hosts this website. Like any web host, it records technical information such as your IP address and browser type in its server logs.
Google Fonts
Supplies the typefaces used on this site. Because the font files are requested from Google's servers, your IP address is visible to Google when a page loads.

We may also share information where we are legally required to do so. Some of these providers operate outside the United Kingdom; where that is the case, transfers are made under the safeguards permitted by UK data protection law.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not lead to work are kept for up to two years, so that we can pick up the conversation if you come back to us. Records relating to clients are kept for longer where we need them for tax, accounting or legal reasons. You can ask us to delete your information sooner at any time.

How it is protected

This website is served over an encrypted connection, and form submissions are sent over encrypted connections to our providers. Access to enquiry emails is limited to people at Malcolm Inc who need to see them.

Cookies

This website does not set any cookies and does not use analytics or advertising trackers. Our Cookie Policy explains this in more detail.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • Ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct anything that is inaccurate or incomplete
  • Ask us to delete your information
  • Ask us to restrict how we use it
  • Object to our use of it where we rely on legitimate interests
  • Ask us to transfer it to you or another organisation
  • Withdraw your consent at any time, where consent is our lawful basis

To exercise any of these, email info@malcominc.co.uk. We will respond within one month. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113, though we would appreciate the chance to put things right first.

Links to other websites

This site may link to other organisations' websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices, and we would encourage you to read their policies.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle personal information we will update this page and change the date at the top.
